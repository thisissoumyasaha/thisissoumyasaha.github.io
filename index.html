01 / What drives me

Frontier models matter when they become useful.

My work spans both sides of AI’s hardest bridge: improving the underlying model, then shaping that capability into something a business can trust and a customer genuinely wants to use.

I work across data curation, synthetic data, chat templates, training recipes, evaluation, and release gating. That breadth lets me connect a model’s behavior back to its training signals, isolate root causes, and turn a research insight into a repeatable system, a reliable product behavior, and ultimately customer value.
